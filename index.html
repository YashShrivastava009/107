Step-by-Step BIS Certification Roadmap

Compliance Completion 0%
Stage 1: Identification & Testing Setup
Establish required in-house testing equipment (Annex B of Product Manual) or tie-up with cluster labs (for MSMEs).[cite: 2]
Stage 2: Online Application (Manakonline)
Submit Form V application on e-BIS with factory layout, test reports of raw materials, and required application fees.
Stage 3: Factory Inspection by BIS Auditor
Technical officer verifies Scheme of Inspection & Testing (SIT), raw material quality, and calibration records.[cite: 2]
Stage 4: Sample Sealing & Third-Party Lab Testing
Independent sample drawn during visit and sent to an accredited NABL lab for complete parameter verification.[cite: 2]
Stage 5: Grant of License (CML Number)
Scrutiny clearance and issuance of BIS Standard Mark (ISI logo) with unique CM/L registration code.[cite: 2]
