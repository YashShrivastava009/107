Standard BIS Scheme-I Certification Journey

End-to-end procedural workflow as per February 2026 Guidelines for Grant and Surveillance.

1

Product Classification & QCO Verification

Check whether the manufactured item falls under mandatory Quality Control Orders (QCO) issued by DPIIT, Ministry of Steel, or MeitY. Determine applicable Indian Standard (IS) and Scheme (Scheme-I, II, or IV).

2

Factory Testing Setup & CBTF Evaluation

Ensure mandatory in-house testing equipment (Scheme of Inspection and Testing - SIT) is calibrated. MSMEs may apply for Cluster Based Test Facility (CBTF) exemptions where applicable.

3

e-BIS (ManakOnline) Application Submission

Submit Form-V online via ManakOnline with factory layout, test reports, machinery details, and statutory application fee (₹1,000).

4

Factory Verification & Sample Sealing

BIS inspecting officer visits premises, checks quality personnel, verifies calibration, inspects raw materials, and draws counter-samples for independent testing.

5

Independent Laboratory Testing (BIS LIMS)

Coded samples are dispatched to accredited BIS / NABL laboratories via the LIMS system for full testing against all specification parameters.

6

Grant of Standard Mark (CM/L Licence)

Upon passing laboratory scrutiny, a unique CM/L (Certification Marks/Licence) number is issued permitting application of the ISI mark.
